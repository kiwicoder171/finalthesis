## 2. The Top 3 Candidate Methods Chosen & Implemented

All three methods inherit from the unified [`BaseECGClassifier`](file:///Users/kiwi/Desktop/Developer/Antigravity/Final-Thesis/src/models/base_classifier.py) interface (**Contract 2**) and consume identical 360-sample R-peak centered heartbeat windows ($[-120, +240]$ samples at $360\text{ Hz}$) plus 4D inter-beat RR rhythm features (`[RR_pre, RR_post, delta_RR, RR_ratio]`) generated by [`src/data/segmenter.py`](file:///Users/kiwi/Desktop/Developer/Antigravity/Final-Thesis/src/data/segmenter.py):

1. **Method A — Engineered Time-Frequency Features + Cost-Sensitive GBDT** ([`src/models/method_a_gbdt.py`](file:///Users/kiwi/Desktop/Developer/Antigravity/Final-Thesis/src/models/method_a_gbdt.py)):
   - **Feature Vector (42 Dimensions)**:
     - *Wavelet Sub-Bands (25 dims)*: 4-level Daubechies `db4` Discrete Wavelet Transform (`cA4, cD4, cD3, cD2, cD1`) extracting energy, relative energy, Shannon entropy, standard deviation, and 90th-percentile magnitude.
     - *Pan-Tompkins & QRS Morphology (13 dims)*: R-peak amplitude, Q-dip, S-dip, T-wave peak, QRS duration/width, QRS area, signal energy, zero-crossing rate, skewness, and kurtosis.
     - *Rhythm Timing Dynamics (4 dims)*: `RR_pre`, `RR_post`, signed `delta_RR`, and `RR_ratio`.
   - **Classifier**: Balanced `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (`max_iter=150`, `learning_rate=0.08`, `l2_regularization=1.0`, inverse class-frequency sample weights).
2. **Method B — Deep 1D Residual Convolutional Network (1D-ResNet)** ([`src/models/method_b_resnet.py`](file:///Users/kiwi/Desktop/Developer/Antigravity/Final-Thesis/src/models/method_b_resnet.py)):
   - **Architecture (1,714,565 parameters)**: Stem Conv ($k=15$) $\to$ 8 Residual Blocks across 3 progressive stages ($64 \to 128 \to 256$ channels, multi-scale kernels $7 \to 5 \to 3$) $\to$ Global Average Pooling (256-D) fused with a 2-layer Rhythm MLP (32-D) $\to$ 288-D classification head.
   - **Loss**: Multi-Class Focal Loss ($\gamma = 2.0$) with balanced class weighting $\alpha_c$.
3. **Method C — Hybrid 1D-CNN + Bidirectional LSTM + Bahdanau Temporal Attention** ([`src/models/method_c_hybrid_attn.py`](file:///Users/kiwi/Desktop/Developer/Antigravity/Final-Thesis/src/models/method_c_hybrid_attn.py)):
   - **Architecture (274,197 parameters)**: 3-layer 1D-CNN local morphological frontend ($k=15 \to 7 \to 5$, compressing 360 samples into 45 temporal tokens) $\to$ 2-layer Bidirectional LSTM ($64 \times 2 = 128$ hidden units capturing forward/backward wave sequence dependencies) $\to$ **Bahdanau Additive Temporal Attention** ($\boldsymbol{\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}^{45}$ focusing on P-wave, QRS complex, and T-wave offsets) $\to$ Late Fusion with Rhythm MLP (16-D) $\to$ LayerNorm classification head.

## 4. Conclusion: What is the Best Method to Use and Why?

### 1. Best Method for Multi-Database & Subtle Ectopic Arrhythmia Detection: **Method C (CNN-BiLSTM-Attention)**
- **Why it wins the Clinical Composite Ranking (`0.5564`)**: Supraventricular Ectopic Beats (**S**) have nearly identical QRS morphology to Normal beats, differing only in a slightly deformed P-wave and premature timing. Because **Method C** pairs local 1D-CNN filters with a Bidirectional LSTM and **Bahdanau Temporal Attention** across 45 time steps, it learns to attend specifically to the P-QRS transition, catching **80.00% of SVEB ($S$) beats** and **89.47% of VEB ($V$) beats** (with **94.92% VEB precision** and **0.9212 VEB F1**), while generalizing best to 12-lead PTB-XL (`Macro F1 = 0.5824`).
- **Why Method B (1D-ResNet) placed 3rd**: With `1.71M` parameters and Global Average Pooling (which destroys temporal ordering across the beat window), 1D-ResNet overfits to training patient morphologies under strict inter-patient splitting when trained without tens of thousands of patient records.

### 2. Best Method for Smartphone Edge Deployment & Overall MIT-BIH DS2 Accuracy: **Method A (GBDT + 42-D Wavelet/Pan-Tompkins/PRV)**
- **Why Method A is the Edge Champion**: On the primary **MIT-BIH DS2 inter-patient test split**, **Method A** achieved the **#1 Macro F1 (`0.5219`)**, **#1 Overall Accuracy (`97.89%`)**, and **#1 Ventricular Ectopic Sensitivity (`91.39%`)**, as well as **#1 Accuracy on 12-lead INCART (`90.2%`)** and **Chapman-Shaoxing (`66.7%`)**.
- **Strategic Takeaway for Our Thesis (`Our work/`)**: Because Daubechies `db4` wavelet sub-band energies and explicit RR-interval ratios are invariant to cross-device amplitude scaling, **Method A** provides the highest precision and accuracy on ambulatory records without requiring PyTorch/GPU runtimes on the user's phone. **Therefore, keeping Method A (GBDT/XGBoost) as our primary on-device thesis classifier—and citing Method C (CNN-BiLSTM-Attention) as the deep spatio-temporal benchmark—is the strongest possible scientific conclusion.**
