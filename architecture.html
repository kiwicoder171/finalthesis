Computer Engineering Capstone Thesis • System Specification

Unified Autonomous Cardiac Risk & Stress Simulation Pipeline

Comprehensive architectural topology for a 4-member integrated system: from real-world electrophysiological acquisition (AD8232), through digital Pan-Tompkins QRS and spectral HRV extraction, down to predictive machine learning risk profiling and dynamic cardiac stress modeling.

Stage 1 • Member A
Electrophysiological Sensing
Captures biological potentials via AD8232 instrumentation amplifier and passes 250Hz sampled integers over Serial to Stage 2.
Stage 2 • Member B (You)
DSP & Feature Extraction
Bandpass conditioning, Pan-Tompkins QRS detection, RR artifact pruning, and clinical HRV feature vector extraction (SDNN, RMSSD, LF/HF).
Stage 3 • Member C
Data Mining & Risk Classifier
Trained on labeled PhysioNet database to predict risk category (Normal vs. Arrhythmia) from Member B's HRV metrics.
Stage 4 • Member D
Dynamical Stress Twin
Nonlinear Van der Pol oscillator driven by Member C's risk score and synthetic stress inputs to simulate rhythm breakdown.
