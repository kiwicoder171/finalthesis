5. Instructions for Member D (Dynamical Twin Simulation)

Member D models the heart as a coupled Van der Pol nonlinear relaxation oscillator:

dx/dt = y
dy/dt = mu * (1 - x^2) * y - omega_0^2 * x + F_stress(t)
